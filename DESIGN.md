# Design System

## Overview

Use the supplied composition: portrait on the left, message on the right, and a curved arrow toward the top right.

## Colors

Use dark ink on a light background. Let the photo supply the color.

## Typography

Use Inter with a system fallback. Use large text and clear line spacing.

## Layout

Keep the desktop composition inside a simple dark frame. Stack the message and portrait on narrow screens.
Keep the arrow near the browser's top-right corner. Allow scrolling at high zoom instead of clipping text.

## Shapes

Use a rounded frame and one curved arrow. Do not add cards, navigation, or unrelated decoration.
